# Marketing-Performance-Insigts
This project analyzes the marketing and sales performance of a multi-category e-commerce business from 2021 to 2023.

The analysis focuses on revenue, customer volume, AOV, revenue per unit, marketing channels, sales volume, and product category performance.

The goal was to understand the key drivers of revenue, identify changes in channel and product performance over time, and find opportunities to support future revenue growth.

## Business Problem
The business maintained relatively stable revenue and customer volume between 2021 and 2023, but this stability did not translate into clear growth.<br>
At the same time, AOV declined from $93 to $91, while revenue per unit decreased from $67 to $66.<br>
The business needed to understand what was driving revenue, how marketing channel performance was changing, and where opportunities existed to increase customer value and support future growth.

## Business Questions
The analysis aimed to answer the following business questions:

How did revenue, customer volume, AOV and revenue per unit change between 2021 and 2023?<br>
Which marketing channels generated the highest revenue and sales volume?<br>
Which channels generated the highest AOV and revenue per unit?<br>
Which product categories contributed the largest share of revenue within each marketing channel?<br>
How did channel and product performance change between 2021 and 2023?<br>
Where are the main opportunities to increase customer value and support future revenue growth?<br>

## Analysis Tasks
The analysis aimed to understand:<br>
Compared revenue, customer volume, AOV and revenue per unit across 2021–2023.<br>
Analyzed revenue and units sold by marketing channel.<br>
Compared AOV and revenue per unit across marketing channels.<br>
Analyzed revenue contribution by product category within each channel.<br>
Compared year-over-year changes in channel and category performance.<br>
Identified revenue drivers, performance trends and potential growth opportunities.<br>

##  Key Insights
1. Stable revenue but limited growth<br>
Revenue remained at approximately $2M annually, while customer volume stayed around 17K per year. However, AOV declined from $93 to $91 (-2.2%), and revenue per unit decreased from $67 to $66 (-1.5%), indicating slightly weaker monetization over time.<br>
2. Customer transaction value slightly declined.<br>
AOV decreased from $93 to $91, while Revenue per Unit declined from $67 to $66. This suggests an opportunity to improve pricing, product mix, cross-selling, upselling, and product bundles.<br>
3. Revenue was largely driven by sales volume.<br>
Channels with higher units sold generally generated higher revenue, indicating that business performance depended more on sales volume than on significant increases in revenue per unit.
4.Electronics generated around 40% of revenue across many channels.<br>
While Electronics is the strongest revenue driver, this also creates product concentration risk. Developing categories such as Home and Fashion could help diversify the revenue mix.

## Recommendations
1.Focus on increasing AOV.<br>
Since AOV declined from $93 to $91, the business could encourage customers to spend more per order through cross-selling, upselling, product bundles, and free-shipping thresholds.
2.Drive revenue growth through value, not only sales volume.<br>
Revenue is currently strongly connected to the number of units sold. The business should also focus on increasing the value of each sale by reviewing pricing, discounts, and product mix.
3.Reduce dependence on Electronics.<br>
Electronics generates around 40% of revenue across many channels, creating a strong dependency on one category. Growing categories such as Home and Fashion through targeted campaigns, cross-category bundles, and personalized offers could create a more balanced revenue mix.
4.Measure channel profitability, not just revenue.<br>
High revenue does not necessarily mean a channel is profitable. Adding Marketing Spend, ROAS, CAC, COGS, Gross Profit, and Gross Margin would help identify which channels and product categories actually create the most value for the business.
